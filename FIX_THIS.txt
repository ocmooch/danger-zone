Original context
-----------------
dz-dashboard renders matchup box scores straight from `team_rosters`. Matchup
193 (2025 wk1, sully [team 4] vs Rob [team 10]) showed wrong players — Brock
Purdy as starting QB for BOTH teams, 2 QB / 2 K / 2 DEF lineups. The dashboard
is NOT at fault; the corruption is in this repo's data + roster loader.

Root cause (confirmed against data/fantasy.db)
----------------------------------------------
2025 wk1 was ingested TWICE (early test load @ 2026-05-29 01:57, full ~193 rows;
historical backfill @ 18:29-18:30, partial ~83 rows). The loader was not
idempotent and the old UNIQUE(team_id, player_id, week) constraint (a) omitted
season_year and (b) was keyed on team_id, so a player who CHANGED teams between
the two snapshots got a new row on the new team while the stale row on the old
team survived → 36 players double-rostered. Only 2025 wk1 is affected.

==========================================================================
PART 1 — root cause — DONE (committed on feature/roster-idempotent-fix)
==========================================================================
* models.py TeamRoster: replaced UNIQUE(team_id, player_id, week) with
  UNIQUE(season_year, week, player_id) = "uq_team_rosters_season_week_player".
  Dropping team_id from the key enforces the real invariant: a player is on at
  most ONE team per scoring week. (A plain UNIQUE *can* span "any team" — it's
  exactly the key that omits team_id. No separate post-load check needed; the
  DB now rejects the violation directly.)
* Loader idempotency (replace-per-scope DELETE + new conflict key) applied to
  ALL THREE roster write paths:
    - league.py  _upsert_team_roster        (live NFL.com scrape)
    - history.py _upsert_lineup_side        (historical weekly backfill)
    - history.py draft capture (week=0)     (conflict-key change only)
  A moved player now UPDATEs onto the new team instead of duplicating.
* alembic migration c4d8a1f6e2b9 (down_revision b3e1d9f4c2a7, the head) swaps
  the constraint via batch_alter_table.
